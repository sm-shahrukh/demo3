TRAVEL HELP

Before you take off

A few useful answers as you plan your next trip.

Back to flight search
How do I search for a flight?

Choose your departure and arrival cities, enter your dates and add the number of travellers. Use Search flights to preview a sample fare where one is available.

What do the Umrah and Visa tabs show?

Umrah shows a sample trip based on your dates and group size. Visa lets you preview an enquiry with your destination and travel details.

Are the prices on this page live?

Prices shown here are examples in PKR. Flight availability, travel dates and package inclusions determine the final price for a real trip.

What does a package include?

Open any escape to see its suggested itinerary. The travel styles section shows examples of flights, accommodation and extras you could combine.

Can I start with a one-way flight?

Yes. Select One way in the search form to plan without a return date.
